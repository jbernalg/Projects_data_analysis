# 🛒 Analysis of the 25 Largest Retailers in the U.S.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a business-focused data analysis of the 25 largest retailers in the United States. The objective is to extract meaningful insights from historical sales and operational data to better understand how different retail companies perform in terms of revenue generation, physical presence, and business category.

Using exploratory data analysis and visualization techniques, this project answers key business questions that allow for performance comparison across companies and help identify operational patterns within the retail industry.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

To analyze how structural factors such as number of stores, company category, and sales distribution influence the financial performance of major retail companies in the U.S.

---

## ❓ Business Questions Addressed

The analysis focuses on answering the following questions:

- Which company sold more?
- What is the average sales excluding the dominant company?
- How much money in sales did most companies generate?
- How many stores do most companies have?
- If a company has more stores, will it have more money from sales?
- What is the range between sales?
- Which 5 companies have the most physical stores? Which of them are in the top 5 companies with the highest sales?
- Which company category generated the most sales?
- Which company generates the most sales in relation to its number of physical stores?
- Which companies sell exclusively online? Do their sales stand out from those who also have physical stores?
- Which companies have had the greatest growth in terms of the number of physical stores?
- Which categories have the highest number of companies?

---

## 🛠️ Analytical Process

The project follows a structured data analysis workflow:

1. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing  
2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
3. Comparative Analysis  
4. Correlation Analysis  
5. Data Visualization  
6. Insight Generation

## 📈 Project Outcome

This analysis provides a comparative view of performance across the largest retailers in the U.S., highlighting how factors such as store count, company category, and sales distribution impact business outcomes. The results support data-driven evaluation of operational strategies within the retail sector.
